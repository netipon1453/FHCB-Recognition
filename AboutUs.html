About Us

Welcome to the Central Islamic Council of Thailand (CICOT).

CICOT is the leading authority responsible for halal certification in Thailand. We work closely with businesses, government agencies, and international partners to ensure that halal standards are met across various industries.

Our mission is to promote and regulate halal certification in Thailand, ensuring that products and services meet the highest standards of quality and compliance of Thai Agricultural Standards: Halal Food (TAS 8400-2025). We are committed to supporting businesses in their halal certification journey and providing consumers with confidence in the products they choose. And also, we are dedicated to promoting and regulating halal certification in Thailand.

Purposes

  • To regulate and standardize halal products and services imported to Thailand by certifying of Foreign Halal Certification Bodies (FHCBS)
  • To assess the compliance of FHCBs with the required standards.
  • To apply TAS 8400-2025 for all halal products imported to Thailand.

How to Apply for FHCB Recognition

To apply for FHCB recognition, interested FHCBs must follow the application process as shown below:

  • Register to obtain an account for submission
  • Complete the application form and submit the required documents. Please follow the application forms on our website step by step.
  • Pay the application fee
  • Verify the payment. If the payment is successful, your application will be processed.
  • Then, your application will be reviewed by our team in detail
  • Undergo the assessment process
  • Receive the final decision on recognition status
  • Your FHCB will be recognized if it meets all the requirements. And also, your FHCB name will be announced in our official website.
  • Recognition certificates will be issued upon successful recognition.

Pivotal Notes

  • FHCB recognition is valid for 3 years. After that, FHCBs must apply for renewal to maintain their recognition status.
  • FHCBs are required to comply with all relevant regulations and standards to maintain their recognition status.
  • In case of FHCBs certifying any product in Thailand, their recognition of CICOT will be revoked automatically.
  • CICOT recognition bases on the country-based criteria. It means that FHCBs must certify halal products within their registered country. If FHCBs would like to certify halal products in other countries outside their original country, they must apply for the different country (each country).
  • Halal certificates issued by recognized FHCBs must be addressed that halal products are certified under TAS 8400-2025, the requirements of CICOT, and the certified scope.
  • Halal certificates must be issued by the registered and recognised country/branch (signed by the authorized representative of the FHCB's branch)
