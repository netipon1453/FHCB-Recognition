The Central Islamic Council of Thailand (CICOT)

Foreign Halal Certification Body (FHCB) Recognition System

CICOT is Thailand's national authority for Islamic affairs and halal certification. Through the FHCB Recognition System, CICOT works with Foreign Halal Certification Bodies around the world to ensure that halal products imported into Thailand meet the Thai Agricultural Standard: Halal Food (TAS 8400-2025).

Our History

The organization and administration of Islamic affairs in Thailand has a long institutional history, evolving over eight decades into the Central Islamic Council of Thailand that operates today.

  • B.E. 2488 (1945): Islamic affairs in Thailand were first formally organized under the Royal Decree on the Patronage of Islam, issued during the reign of King Rama VIII. The decree established a three-tier structure consisting of the Central Islamic Committee, Provincial Islamic Committees, and Mosque Islamic Committees.
  • B.E. 2540 (1997): The Administration of Islamic Organization Act B.E. 2540 was enacted, reorganizing the national structure and granting the Central Islamic Council of Thailand legal status as a religious juristic person. Under this Act, the Sheikhul Islam of Thailand (Chularajmontri) serves as Chairman of the Council, with board members representing all 36 provincial Islamic committees, each appointed by the King for a six-year term.
  • 2001: CICOT formalized its Halal certification regulations, establishing a common national standard for Halal products and services across Thailand.
  • 2009: Comprehensive regulations governing Halal product quality control and the use of the Halal logo were issued, strengthening the credibility of Thailand's Halal certification system.
  • Present day: CICOT continues to serve as Thailand's national authority for Halal certification, advising government agencies on Islamic affairs, overseeing Islamic legal matters, and recognizing Foreign Halal Certification Bodies under TAS 8400-2025.

Our Purpose

  • To regulate and standardize halal products and services imported to Thailand by certifying Foreign Halal Certification Bodies (FHCBs).
  • To assess the compliance of FHCBs with the required standards.
  • To apply TAS 8400-2025 for all halal products imported to Thailand.
